# Gabrious Enterprise Features

This document outlines the advanced features available for enterprise customers of Gabrious.

## API Access

API access allows you to integrate Gabrious' sermon transcription and study notes generation capabilities directly into your existing systems and workflows.

- **RESTful API**: Access all Gabrious functionality programmatically
- **Authentication**: Secure API keys for your organization
- **Rate Limits**: Higher rate limits for enterprise customers
- **Webhooks**: Get notified when transcriptions and study notes are complete

## Batch Processing

Batch processing enables efficient handling of multiple sermons at once, perfect for churches with archives or regular sermon series.

- **Bulk Upload**: Process multiple sermons in a single operation
- **Scheduled Processing**: Set up automatic processing for new content
- **Series Management**: Group and organize sermons by series or events
- **Batch Export**: Export multiple transcriptions and study notes at once

## Custom Branding

Custom branding allows you to maintain your church or ministry's identity throughout the study materials.

- **Logo Integration**: Add your logo to all generated materials
- **Custom Colors**: Match your brand's color scheme
- **Custom Domain**: Access Gabrious through your own branded URL
- **Customized Templates**: Tailor the layout and design of study notes to match your style

## Priority Support

Enterprise customers receive enhanced support options to ensure smooth operation.

- **Dedicated Account Manager**: Personal point of contact for your account
- **Priority Queue**: Faster response times for support inquiries
- **Phone Support**: Direct line to technical assistance
- **Training Sessions**: Personalized onboarding and training for your team
- **SLA Guarantees**: Service level agreements for enterprise customers
